# Independent review v1: Claude Duet promo (57 s)

**Verdict.** The spine works and the Reply card and morning brief read well. But the first 20 s are small and slow, three beats that should be the most energetic are near-empty paper, and the calendar, dashboard and prototype shots are cropped, garbled or illegible. Fix items 1-6 before shipping.

## Problems, most severe first

1. **0-19 s: tiny subject, no hook.** 0-2 s is bare paper, a speck and a ~24 px "you". Coins are ~120 px and badges ~4 px, so the pressure of "Every app wants you" is invisible; the coil reads as a spring. "Claude Duet" first appears at 53 s. Fix: start already moving, big badge numbers ticking up, camera ~1.5x closer, setup of 12 s or less.

2. **Dead beats: ~22.5-23.5 s (dive), 35-36.5 s (whip), 43-45 s (after approve).** The dive lands on blank paper with a red "!" and a ragged beige blob that looks like a stain. The whip is blank paper plus one thin line. After approve, only paper, a line and a Gmail coin cut by the bottom edge remain. Fix: keep an object in frame, start the next stroke early, grow the wash as the card's own rounded shape.

3. **30-35 s "Meetings. Dashboards. Prototypes." shows none of the three clearly.** No calendar grid appears in any sampled frame. Bars are cut by the left edge, untitled, with a ~30 px skewed label. The 32 s morph is garbled translucent facets; the wireframe is empty when the caption fades. Fix: one centred, uncropped ~2 s beat per word: calendar with a large "Thu 2:00 Dana", titled chart, wireframe filled with blocks.

4. **Approve tick (40.5-41.8 s).** A black stroke runs diagonally across "Approve" and "Friday works.", reading as scribble; the camera leaves ~1 s later. "You decide." fades by ~43.7 s (under 2 s). Fix: compact terracotta tick at the button's right end, clear of text; hold 1.2 s; caption fully drawn 2.2 s or more.

5. **Braid (45-50 s).** A pale cool-white horizon band tops the frame, a hue and brightness jump from the cream. Coins float edge-on (logos unreadable), Slack covers Calendar. The helix shadow sits on "One duet." and is cut by the bottom edge; the braid fills only the left third. Fix: limit camera pitch, coins in a calm orbit facing camera, braid centred, out of the bottom 15%.

6. **End card (51-57 s).** The tagline is ~20 px light grey, illegible on a phone; the lockup is crammed low. A thin grey tail from the frame edge touches the logo for the whole hold. The icon's grey squiggle is far lighter than the black braid, so the handoff reads as a fade. Fix: icon 1.3x and centred, tagline 40 px or more and darker, retract the tail before 53 s.

7. **Smudges and tiny text.** The pen shadow is a hard grey streak (top right 0-17 s, over "Morning brief" and "Yes, Friday works."): soften, shorten, hide in wide shots. At 360 px the "Claude" label, "6,000+ connectors" (15-19 s) and Claude's 2-3 px ring vanish: make "6,000+ connectors" a real caption. The morning-brief wash is a muddy mustard slab; lighten it.

## Requirement violations
- **Clean:** violated (2, 3, 5-7).
- **Dynamic and tense:** weak at 0-20 s and 43-45 s.
- **Show what each drawing becomes:** violated (calendar, dashboard, prototype).
- **More colour:** partial; washes and bars are desaturated.
- **Real apps:** unreadable when small or edge-on; Docs and Calendar look alike.
- **Apple-like transitions:** dive and whip cross empty paper; horizon band jumps brightness.
- **Text readable ~2 s:** "You decide.", "6,000+ connectors" fail.
- **English only, official logo:** pass.

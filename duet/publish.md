# 发布文案（v2《The Ride》）

**标题**

Claude Duet ·《The Ride》

**正文（中文）**

所有 app 都在找你。
你和 Claude 坐进同一辆车，两支笔边走边画出脚下的轨道：早上的晨报、这一周的会、要看的图表。
到了要替你回信的时候，车停在最高点，等你点头。你点下去，剩下的它来。
一整天坐完，从高处回头看——这条轨道，画的就是 Duet。

（Claude Duet 是 Anthropic 2026 年 10 月 21 日开放的 research preview，目前在 waitlist，先给美国 Pro 和 Max 用户。片中的场景是示意。）

**Caption (English)**

Every app wants you. You and Claude ride together, drawing the track as you go: the morning brief, the week, the dashboards. At the top, it stops and asks before it acts. You decide. It does the rest.
Your whole day. One line.

Claude Duet is a research preview from Anthropic. Join the waitlist (starting with US Pro and Max subscribers). Scenes in the film are illustrative.
